The training

What you train

  • Technique

    Stance, footwork, punches, kicks, knees, elbows and clinch. Step by step, with attention to detail.

  • Padwork

    Rounds on the pads with direct feedback, so technique becomes timing and power.

  • Conditioning

    Fight conditioning that makes you fitter and sharper, whether you compete or not.

  • Strength for fighters

    Strength & conditioning that supports your Muay Thai: more power, better balance, fewer weak spots.

Absolutely. I coach complete beginners to experienced fighters, step by step.
