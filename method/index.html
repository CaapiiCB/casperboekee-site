Your trajectory

Step by step

  1. 1

    Free intro

    30 minutes at Patrick's Gym. We talk about your goal, your background and any injuries, and see if we're a match.

  2. 2

    Intake and assessment

    You fill in a short intake form. In the first session I test your starting point: movement, mobility, strength and conditioning.

  3. 3

    Your training plan

    Based on your goal and the assessment you get a personal training plan, on your phone, with sets, reps and weights.

  4. 4

    Train and track

    We train 1-on-1. I record what you lift and how it felt, so every session builds on the last one.

  5. 5

    Re-test and adjust

    Every 6 to 8 weeks we re-test. You see what has improved and we adjust the plan for the next block.

Personal training

A personal training session

60 minutes, fully 1-on-1. Every session follows the same structure, adjusted to you.

01

Check-in

5 min

How did you sleep, how is your energy and do you feel any pain? If needed, I adjust the session on the spot.

02

Warm-up and activation

10 min

Mobility and activation exercises that fit your body and your assessment.

03

Strength

30 min

The main part of the session. I coach your technique on every set and keep track of your weights.

04

Core and conditioning

10 min

Core stability, carries and conditioning, so you get stronger as a whole.

05

Wrap-up

5 min

What went well, what's next, and what to do if you train on your own.

Muay Thai

A Muay Thai session

60 minutes, fully 1-on-1. Technique first, then we put it to work on the pads.

01

Warm-up

10 min

Skipping or shadowboxing and mobility for hips and shoulders.

02

Technique

15 min

One theme per session: stance and footwork, combinations, kicks, knees, elbows or clinch.

03

Padwork

20 min

Rounds of 2 to 3 minutes on the pads with direct feedback, so technique turns into timing and power.

04

Conditioning

10 min

Bag work or fight-specific conditioning, adjusted to your level.

05

Cool-down

5 min

Calm down, mobility, and a short review of the session.

Times are a guide. Every session is adjusted to your level, your goal and how you feel that day.
