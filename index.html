About me

I'm Casper.

I'm a certified personal trainer and fitness instructor from Amsterdam (Fitvak A&B, and currently completing the NASM CPT). I combine Muay Thai, strength & conditioning and functional performance training to help you build real power, endurance and conditioning.

My goal is for you to become stronger, fitter, more confident and more disciplined, physically and mentally. I coach complete beginners as well as experienced fighters, and everyone gets a plan with progress you can measure.

You'll find me at Patrick's Gym in Amsterdam, where I've been coaching personal training, strength & conditioning and Muay Thai for over two years.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What results can I expect?

You get stronger, fitter and move with less pain, with progress you can measure. We test at the start and re-test regularly, so you see it in numbers.

Is the training 1-on-1?

Yes. Every session is 1-on-1, tailored to your goal and level.

What does the coaching look like?

You get a clear plan instead of random workouts. We start with an intro and an assessment, then train 1-on-1 and re-test regularly, so your progress stays visible.

Do you offer Muay Thai sessions?

Yes. You can book Muay Thai on its own or combine it with strength training, depending on your goal.

What can I expect from a Muay Thai session?

Technique, padwork and conditioning, built up step by step. You learn to punch, kick, knee and elbow properly, and you get fitter while doing it.

Is Muay Thai suitable for beginners?

Absolutely. I coach complete beginners to experienced fighters, step by step.

Where do the sessions take place?

At Patrick's Gym, Marius Bauerstraat 28, 1062 AR Amsterdam.

How do I book a session?

How do I pay?
