Why 1-on-1

The best way to start

  • Your own pace

    No group to keep up with. We take the time you need for every technique.

  • Good technique from day one

    Direct feedback on every punch and kick, so you don't learn bad habits.

  • No experience needed

    We start with stance and footwork and build up from there.

  • Get fit along the way

    Muay Thai trains your whole body: conditioning, strength and coordination.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Muay Thai suitable for beginners?

Absolutely. I coach complete beginners to experienced fighters, step by step.

Do I need to be fit to start?

No. We start at your current level and build up from there. The plan is made for where you are now, not where you think you should be.

What can I expect from a Muay Thai session?

Technique, padwork and conditioning, built up step by step. You learn to punch, kick, knee and elbow properly, and you get fitter while doing it.

What should I bring?

Comfortable sports clothes, training shoes, a water bottle and a towel.

What does personal training cost?

A single 60-minute session is €85. A package of 10 sessions is €850, including an assessment at the start, a personal training plan and re-tests along the way.
