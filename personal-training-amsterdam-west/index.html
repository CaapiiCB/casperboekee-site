FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where do the sessions take place?

At Patrick's Gym, Marius Bauerstraat 28, 1062 AR Amsterdam.

What happens during the free intro?

We meet for 30 minutes at Patrick's Gym. We talk about your goal, your training background and any injuries, and I explain how I would approach it. There's no obligation to continue.

How often should I train?

Most clients train with me one to three times a week, sometimes combined with sessions on their own. During the intro we decide what fits your goal and your schedule.

What does personal training cost?

A single 60-minute session is €85. A package of 10 sessions is €850, including an assessment at the start, a personal training plan and re-tests along the way. Prices include VAT.

Do you coach in English?

Yes. I coach in both Dutch and English.
