The assessment

What we test

01

Pre-training screening

A standard check before we start training, just like in any professional gym.

  • Resting heart rate
  • Blood pressure
  • Height and body weight

02

Body composition

Measured with a skinfold caliper and tape, not estimated by a scale.

  • Body fat percentage (skinfold measurement)
  • Circumferences: waist, hips, chest, arms and thighs
  • Waist-to-hip ratio

03

Posture

A static posture analysis of five key areas.

  • Head position
  • Shoulders
  • Upper back
  • Pelvic position
  • Alignment of legs and feet

04

Movement & mobility

How well you move, measured in degrees with a goniometer.

  • Overhead squat assessment
  • Shoulder mobility
  • Hip mobility
  • Hamstring flexibility
  • Ankle mobility

05

Strength & stability

Tested at your level, left and right.

  • Grip strength with a hand dynamometer
  • Single-leg balance
  • Core stability
  • Strength tests suited to your level

06

Conditioning

A cardio test with live heart rate monitoring (Polar H10).

  • Cardio test with heart rate zones
  • Heart rate recovery
  • Estimated VO2max

The Performance Scan is a fitness and performance assessment, not a medical examination, and it doesn't give a diagnosis. If a measurement stands out, I'll advise you to check with your GP before we start training.

What you get

Results you can act on

  • Personal report

    All your results in one clear report, compared with fitness norms for your age and sex.

  • Top 3 priorities

    What matters most for your goal right now, and why.

  • Training plan

    Concrete training recommendations you can start with straight away.

  • Review call

    20 to 30 minutes to go through your report and options together.
