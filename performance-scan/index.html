The assessment

What we measure

  • Body composition

    Weight, body fat percentage and circumferences.

  • Movement quality

    Overhead squat assessment, mobility and posture.

  • Strength and stability

    Grip strength, balance, core and strength tests at your level.

    Resting heart rate, blood pressure and a cardio test with heart rate monitoring.
